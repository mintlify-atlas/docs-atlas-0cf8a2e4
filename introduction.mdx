Discord Tickets is the most popular open-source ticket management bot for Discord. It is a free alternative to the premium and white-label plans of other ticket management bots, trusted by thousands of servers with over half a million tickets created since 2019.

## What is Discord Tickets?

Discord Tickets gives your Discord server a fully featured support ticket system. Members open tickets through buttons or select menus, staff respond inside private channels, and everything is archived for later review. You own the bot and the data.

## Ways to use Discord Tickets

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Public bot" icon="bolt">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Managed bot" icon="cloud">
    Your own dedicated bot, hosted by the project maintainer. Easy setup, low cost, and no server required on your end.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Self-hosted" icon="server">

  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Current version

The current release is **v4.0.50**. Discord Tickets v4 uses discord.js v14 and requires Node.js 18 or later.
