---
title: "/close"
description: "Request that the current ticket be closed."
---

The `/close` command submits a request to close the ticket in the current channel. Depending on how the server is configured, the close may require approval from a staff member or from the ticket creator before the channel is deleted.

## Who can use it

All server members can use `/close` inside a ticket channel they have access to.

## Options

<ParamField query="reason" type="string" optional>
  The reason for closing the ticket. This is recorded in the close event and
  may be included in the transcript and log channel.
</ParamField>

## Example usage

```
/close
```

```
/close reason:Issue resolved, no further action needed
```

## What happens after you run it

- **If staff approval is required:** a message is posted in the ticket asking staff to accept or reject the close request.
- **If user approval is required:** a message is sent asking the ticket creator to confirm.
- **If no approval is required:** the ticket is closed immediately and the channel is deleted after a short delay.

<Note>
  To close a ticket immediately without waiting for approval — or to close
  tickets in bulk — use [`/force-close`](/commands/slash/force-close) (staff
  only).
</Note>

<Warning>
  Once a ticket is closed and the channel is deleted, the conversation is no
  longer accessible through Discord. Use [`/transcript`](/commands/slash/transcript)
  beforehand if you need a copy.
</Warning>
