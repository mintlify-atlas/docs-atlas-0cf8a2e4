---
title: "/tickets"
description: "List your open and closed tickets, or view another member's tickets."
---

The `/tickets` command displays a summary of a member's ticket history, including all currently open tickets and up to the 10 most recently closed tickets.

## Who can use it

- **All members** — can view their own tickets.
- **Staff members** — can view tickets belonging to any member, filtered to categories where they have a staff role. Members with the **Manage Server** permission (or configured super-users) can view tickets across all categories.

## Options

<ParamField query="member" type="user" optional>
  The member whose tickets you want to list. Defaults to yourself when omitted.
  Only staff members can specify another member.
</ParamField>

## Example usage

```
/tickets
```

```
/tickets member:@Dave
```

## What happens after you run it

You receive an ephemeral embed showing:

- **Open tickets** — a list of currently open ticket channels with their topics (truncated to 30 characters).
- **Closed tickets** — up to 10 recently closed tickets, each showing the category name, ticket number, channel ID, and topic.

If archiving is enabled on the server, the embed description includes a link to use [`/transcript`](/commands/slash/transcript) to download a ticket's transcript.

<Note>
  The closed tickets list is limited to the 10 most recent entries. To browse
  older tickets, use the bot's web portal.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Non-staff members who attempt to view another member's tickets will receive
  an error.
</Warning>
