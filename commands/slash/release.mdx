---
title: "/release"
description: "Release (unclaim) a ticket so another staff member can take it."
---

The `/release` command removes the claim on the current ticket, making it available for other staff members to claim.

## Who can use it

Staff members only.

## Options

This command has no options.

## Example usage

```
/release
```

## What happens after you run it

- The claim assignment is removed from the ticket.
- A message is posted in the ticket channel: *"@You has released this ticket."*
- The ticket returns to the general staff pool.

<Note>
  You must run `/release` from inside the ticket channel you want to unclaim.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use [`/claim`](/commands/slash/claim) to reassign the ticket to yourself or
  ask another staff member to claim it after releasing.
</Tip>
