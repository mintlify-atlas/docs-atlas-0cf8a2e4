---
title: "Create your first ticket category"
description: "Set up a ticket category in the dashboard so members can open their first ticket."
---

After the bot is running and has joined your server, you need to create at least one ticket category. A category defines where tickets are created, who can access them, and what information is collected from the member before a ticket is opened.

## Prerequisites

- The bot is running and online in your server
- You are listed in the `SUPER` environment variable, or you have the **Administrator** permission in the server
- You have completed the [invite](/getting-started/invite) step

## Open the dashboard

Navigate to the URL you set as `HTTP_EXTERNAL` in your browser. For example:

```
https://tickets.example.com
```

Sign in with Discord using the **Login** button. After authorising, you will be redirected to the dashboard.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select your server">
    On the dashboard home page, select your server from the list of guilds you manage.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Go to Categories">
    In the server settings sidebar, click **Categories**, then click **New category**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the category">
    Fill in the category settings:

    - **Name** — a short label used in the panel button and ticket channel names (for example, `Support`)
    - **Channel** — the Discord channel where the panel (button) will appear
    - **Claiming** — whether staff can claim exclusive ownership of tickets
    - **Topic** — optional; if enabled, the member must choose a topic before the ticket is created
    - **Questions** — optional; up to 5 custom questions shown in a modal before the ticket is created

    You can also configure:

    - **Opening message** — the first message posted inside every new ticket
    - **Ping roles** — roles to notify when a new ticket is opened
    - **Cooldown** — how long a member must wait between opening tickets in this category
    - **Maximum open tickets** — limit on how many tickets one member can have open at a time
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save and send the panel">
    Click **Save**. Then click **Send panel** to post the ticket creation button in the channel you selected.

    Members can now click the button to open a ticket in this category.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Testing the category

Once the panel is posted, open a ticket yourself to verify everything is working:

1. Click the button in the panel channel.
2. Complete any topic selection or questions you configured.
3. Confirm a private ticket channel is created and the opening message appears.
4. Close the ticket using the `/close` command or the close button.
