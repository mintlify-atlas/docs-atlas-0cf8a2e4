---
title: "Create a Discord bot"
description: "Register a Discord application and get a bot token from the Developer Portal."
---

Before you can run Discord Tickets, you need to register a Discord application and create a bot user. This gives you the bot token and OAuth2 credentials the bot needs.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Discord Developer Portal">
    Go to [discord.com/developers/applications](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and sign in with your Discord account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create a new application">
    Click **New Application** in the top-right corner. Give it a name (this is your bot's display name) and click **Create**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the application">
    On the **General Information** page you can set:

    - **Name** — shown in the Discord UI
    - **Description** — shown on the bot's profile
    - **App Icon** — the bot's avatar

    Take note of the **Application ID** on this page — you will need it to build the invite URL.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the bot user">
    In the left sidebar, click **Bot**.

    Click **Add Bot**, then confirm. A bot user is now attached to your application.

    <Warning>
      Do not share your bot token. Anyone with the token has full control over your bot. If it is ever exposed, reset it immediately using the **Reset Token** button.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the bot token">
    Under the bot's username, click **Reset Token** (or **Copy** if shown) to reveal the token. Copy it and store it securely — you will set it as `DISCORD_TOKEN` in your environment.

    <Note>
      You only see the token once. If you navigate away before copying it, you must reset it to get a new one.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable required intents">
    On the **Bot** page, scroll down to **Privileged Gateway Intents** and enable:

    - **Server Members Intent** — required to look up member information
    - **Message Content Intent** — required for tag matching and reading ticket messages

    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Get the OAuth2 credentials">
    In the left sidebar, click **OAuth2**.

    Copy the **Client ID** (same as the Application ID) and the **Client Secret**. Store the Client Secret securely — you will set it as `DISCORD_SECRET` in your environment.

    Add a redirect URL under **Redirects**. The redirect URL is your `HTTP_EXTERNAL` value followed by `/auth/callback`. For example:

    ```
    https://tickets.example.com/auth/callback
    ```

    Click **Save Changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you need from this step

After completing the steps above, you should have:

| Value | Environment variable |
|---|---|
| Bot token | `DISCORD_TOKEN` |
| OAuth2 client secret | `DISCORD_SECRET` |

You will use these in the [configuration](/self-hosting/configuration) step.
