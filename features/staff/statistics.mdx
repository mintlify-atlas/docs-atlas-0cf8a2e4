Discord Tickets records timing and quality metrics for every ticket. These metrics are available in the web dashboard, surfaced as variables in the opening message template, and included in transcripts.

## Tracked metrics

### First response time

When a staff member sends the first message in a ticket, the timestamp is stored as `firstResponseAt`. The **average response time** is the mean of `(firstResponseAt − createdAt)` across all closed tickets in a set.

### Resolution time

The **average resolution time** is the mean of `(closedAt − createdAt)` across all closed tickets that have a `firstResponseAt` value (i.e. tickets that received at least one staff reply).

### Feedback rating

The **average rating** is the mean of all `feedback.rating` values (1–5) across closed tickets that have feedback. Tickets without a rating are excluded from the calculation.

### Message count

Every message sent in a ticket channel increments a `messageCount` counter per user in the `User` table. The total message count across all users is exposed in the client API stats.

## Calculation details

All average calculations are performed in a dedicated worker thread (`stats` pool) to avoid blocking the main event loop.

| Metric | Formula | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `avgResponseTime` | `mean(firstResponseAt − createdAt)` | milliseconds (displayed as human-readable string) |
| `avgResolutionTime` | `mean(closedAt − createdAt)` | milliseconds (displayed as human-readable string) |
| `avgRating` | `mean(feedback.rating)` where rating exists | number, displayed to 1 decimal place |

Only tickets with a non-null `firstResponseAt` are included in resolution and response time calculations. This excludes tickets that were closed without ever receiving a staff reply.

## Houston reporting

Once per day the bot reports anonymous aggregated statistics to Houston (the Discord Tickets telemetry service at `stats.discordtickets.app`). The payload includes:

- Per-guild averages for response and resolution time (in minutes)
- Total ticket counts and enabled feature flags (claiming, feedback, questions, tags, auto-close, working hours)
- Anonymised guild ID (MD5 hash)
- Bot version, Node.js version, OS, and database provider

No user-identifiable information is included. Guild IDs are hashed before transmission.

<Note>
Houston reporting failures are logged as warnings and are non-fatal — the bot continues operating normally if the report cannot be delivered.
</Note>
